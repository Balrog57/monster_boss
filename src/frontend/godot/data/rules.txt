Boss Monster - Official Rules (digest built into the game)
Sources: 10th anniversary rulebook (docs/rules/rules.md) and the wiki
expansions (assets/data/expansions/). The in-app store of the original
APK has been replaced by these rules; everything is unlocked.

OBJECT OF THE GAME
You are a Boss building a dungeon to lure Heroes in and steal their
Souls. The first player to reach 10 SOULS wins. A Hero who survives your
dungeon wounds you: at 5 WOUNDS you are eliminated.
Tie on Souls: the best score (Souls - Wounds) wins, then the Boss with
the lowest XP.

SETUP
- Heroes: 2 players = 13 ordinary + 8 epic; 3 players = 17 + 12;
  4 players = 25 + 16. Epic Heroes show up once the ordinary ones run
  out (they are worth 2 Souls but inflict 2 Wounds).
- Each player picks 1 Boss, receives 5 Rooms + 2 Spells, discards 2
  cards, then builds 1 first ordinary Room.

THE 5 PHASES
1. BEGINNING: 1 Hero per player arrives in town, everyone draws 1 Room.
2. BUILD: in XP order, each player builds 1 Room face-down (max 5
   visible Rooms). An ordinary Room goes at the entrance or on top of
   any Room. An ADVANCED Room (gold icon) can only be built ON a Room
   sharing at least one treasure. Rooms are revealed simultaneously.
3. BAIT: each Hero heads for the dungeon with the most of ITS treasure
   (Cleric / Fighter / Mage / Thief). A tie keeps the Hero in town.
4. ADVENTURE: in XP order, each Hero crosses the Rooms from the
   entrance. A Room deals damage. 0 HP = dead = SOUL. A survivor wounds
   the Boss: 1 Wound (2 for an epic Hero).
5. END: 10 Souls / 5 Wounds are checked, otherwise next turn.

HOW TO PLAY
- Click a Room card in your hand to build it, or drag it onto your
  dungeon.
- Click a Spell card in your hand to play it. If it needs a target, a
  window asks you to choose one.
- Click a glowing Room in your dungeon to use its ability.
- During the Adventure, press GO to move the Hero one Room forward.
  PASS TURN only shows up when you can still play a Spell or an ability.

BOSS ABILITIES
Your "Level Up" ability triggers the first time your dungeon reaches 5
visible Rooms.

SPELLS
Hammer icon = Build phase. Axe icon = Adventure phase.
Effects resolve "last played, first resolved".

EXPANSIONS
From the wiki: The Next Level, Crash Landing, Minibosses + Tools, Hidden Heroes,
Players' Choice. Enable them from the menu: extra Bosses, Rooms, Spells
and Heroes join the decks.

MULTIPLAYER
Single player = you + AI. Hotseat = 2 to 4 humans on the same screen
(PLAYER 1..4 buttons), no AI. Online play with friends uses a private
lobby code.
