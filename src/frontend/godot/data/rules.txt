**Boss Monster - Master of the Dungeon**

Build the deadliest dungeon, lure the Heroes in and steal their Souls.

All decks and expansions are unlocked and free.

OBJECT OF THE GAME

You are a Boss building a dungeon to lure Heroes in and steal their Souls. The first player to reach **10 Souls** wins. A Hero who survives your dungeon wounds you: at **5 Wounds** you are eliminated.

Tie on Souls: the best score (Souls - Wounds) wins, then the Boss with the lowest XP.

SETUP

- **Heroes**: 2 players = 13 ordinary + 8 epic; 3 players = 17 + 12; 4 players = 25 + 16. Epic Heroes show up once the ordinary ones run out (they are worth 2 Souls but inflict 2 Wounds).

- **Start**: each player is dealt a random Boss, receives 5 Rooms + 2 Spells, discards 2 cards, then builds a first Room (face-down, revealed once everyone pressed DONE).

THE 5 PHASES

1. **Beginning**: 1 Hero per player arrives in town, everyone draws 1 Room.

2. **Build**: in XP order, each player builds 1 Room face-down (max 5 visible Rooms). An ordinary Room goes at the entrance or on top of any Room. An advanced Room (gold icon) can only be built on a Room sharing at least one treasure. Rooms are revealed simultaneously.

3. **Bait**: each Hero heads for the dungeon with the most of its treasure (Cleric / Fighter / Mage / Thief). A tie keeps the Hero in town.

4. **Adventure**: in XP order, each Hero crosses the Rooms from the entrance. A Room deals damage. 0 HP = dead = a Soul for you. A survivor wounds the Boss: 1 Wound (2 for an epic Hero).

5. **End**: 10 Souls / 5 Wounds are checked, otherwise next turn.

HOW TO PLAY

- **Build a Room**: click a Room card in your hand, or drag it onto your dungeon. Until you press **DONE** you can swap it for another Room from your hand. DONE ends your build turn.

- **Cast a Spell**: click a Spell card in your hand to play it. If it needs a target, a window asks you to choose one.

- **Use an ability**: click a glowing Room in your dungeon.

- **Adventure**: press **GO** to move the Hero one Room forward. **PASS TURN** only shows up when you can still play a Spell or an ability.

BOSS ABILITIES

Your "Level Up" ability triggers the first time your dungeon reaches 5 visible Rooms.

SPELLS

Hammer icon = Build phase. Axe icon = Adventure phase.

Effects resolve "last played, first resolved".

EXPANSIONS

The Next Level, Crash Landing and Rise of the Minibosses (from the Boss Monster wiki) plus Tools of Hero-Kind, Hidden Heroes and The Player's Choice. Pick them on the expansion screen before a game.

MULTIPLAYER

Single player = you + AI. Hotseat = 2 to 4 humans on the same screen (PLAYER 1..4 buttons), no AI. Online play with friends uses a private lobby code.
